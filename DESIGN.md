## Brand & Style

This design system drives a high-velocity Indonesian flash-sale marketplace. The visual narrative fuses pulsating retail urgency with structural institutional trust, preventing the frantic nature of flash promotions from degrading into visual chaos or perceived insecurity.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
The style anchors on a hybrid of **High-Contrast Bold Commerce** and **Modern FinTech Clarity**. 
- **High-Velocity Urgency:** Tactile ticker badges, high-impact deal counters, and razor-sharp pricing blocks ignite instantaneous purchase intent.
- **Institutional Trust:** Rigorous structural grids, precise micro-borders, clean neutral grounding, and deliberate whitespace provide the balance and reassurance of a certified banking utility.
- **Cultural Familiarity:** Tuned for the vibrant, mobile-first Indonesian digital ecosystem, optimizing for hyper-legible pricing, structured badge signifiers, and thumb-friendly touch targets.

## Colors

The palette leverages a high-voltage primary crimson to communicate immediate action, countered by deep midnight navy for grounded readability and authority. Soft neutral tiers isolate high-saturation deal surfaces.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#D70324`):** Urgent action drivers, primary CTA buttons, active timer highlights, high-tier discount badges.
- **Secondary / Deep Navy (`#14213D`):** Primary typography, high-priority headings, solid contrast iconography, and structural dark-mode accents.
- **Tertiary / Warm Radiant Orange (`#FF6B35`):** Supplemental energetic accents, limited-stock warnings, and F&B vertical highlights.
- **Neutral Core (`#6C757D`):** Secondary meta-text, subtle borders, unfilled tracking indicators, and deactivated states.

### Category Accent Tokens
To establish rapid cross-vertical recognition within horizontal deal feeds, vertical accents must be used strictly for badges, category chips, card tag ribbons, and contextual indicators:
- **Food & Beverage:** `#FF6B35` (Warm energetic orange)
- **Fashion & Lifestyle:** `#E01A67` (Vivid magenta)
- **Electronics & Gadgets:** `#0066FF` (Vibrant tech blue)

### Surface & Background Tokens
- **Canvas Base:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white for modal sheets, interactive card faces, and text fields)
- **Surface Muted:** `#F8F9FA` (Global screen canvas background, neutral containers)
- **Surface Subtle:** `#F1F3F5` (Card divider tracks, deactivated pill backgrounds, timer unit cells)
- **Border Default:** `#E9ECEF` (Low-contrast containment lines for cards and forms)
- **Border Active:** `#CED4DA` (Field focus boundaries and inactive counter strokes)

## Typography

The typography relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, utilizing its geometric proportions, open apertures, and crisp letterforms for rapid parsing on dense mobile displays.

### Numeric Formatting Rules
- **Indonesian Rupiah Display:** Currency symbol (`Rp`) must always sit adjacent to the amount without a full space or separated by a thin non-breaking space (e.g., `Rp15.000` or `Rp99.000`). Indonesian dot separators for thousands are strictly enforced.
- **Original Price Strikes:** Render original values in `price-original` using `#6C757D` with a line-through, stacked or horizontally adjacent to the emphasized `price-md` or `price-xl`.
- **Countdown Numerals:** Always set numbers in tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) inside badge blocks to prevent visual jitter when seconds tick down.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is optimized around mobile velocity and rapid horizontal discovery, transitioning to a structural multi-column setup on larger viewports.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Logic
- **Mobile (<768px):** Fluid single or dual-column layout. Margin is locked to `1rem` (16px), gutters at `1rem` (16px). Voucher and deal cards standardly display in a 2-column grid (`grid-cols-2`) with an aspect ratio tuned for vertical density.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 6-column grid with `1.5rem` outer margins. Voucher cards reflow into a 3-column configuration.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column fixed-max grid (maximum container width `1200px`) centered with `2rem` margins. Flash sale matrices span 4 columns per deal card.

### Structural Flow
- Interactive button heights are locked to a minimum of 48px on mobile views to ensure thumb reachability.
- Horizontal product carousels ("Flash Hour") bleed edge-to-edge using negative margin offsets with 16px internal padding for fluid touch scrubbing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on deep blur skeuomorphism. Instead, it balances crisp structural borders with ambient, colored under-glows for high-priority sales triggers.

### Layer Hierarchy
1. **Flat Base (Canvas / Neutral):** Background surfaces (`#F8F9FA`) contain zero shadow and utilize border outlines (`#E9ECEF`) to define separation.
2. **Resting Deal Cards:** `box-shadow: 0 2px 4px rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.02)`. Grounded, clean, bounded by a 1px solid border (`#E9ECEF`).
3. **Elevated & Hover States:** `box-shadow: 0 8px 16px rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.08), 0 2px 4px rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.04)`. Card translates -2px on the Y-axis.
4. **Urgent Action / Sticky CTA Bar:** `box-shadow: 0 -4px 12px rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.06)`. Sits fixed on bottom viewport with pure white background.
5. **Flash Urgency Glow (Badges & Hero CTAs):** `box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(215, 3, 36, 0.25)`. Reserved strictly for primary red action buttons and high-discount percentage flags.

## Shapes

The design uses a standard rounded geometry (`roundedness: 2`, where default elements leverage 12px radius through `rounded-xl`), creating friendly yet controlled corners that prevent visual fatigue in dense grid layouts.

### Radius Assignments
- **Cards, Input Groups, Base Modals:** 12px (`rounded-xl`).
- **Standard Primary & Secondary Buttons:** 12px (`rounded-xl`).
- **Category Icons, Deal Media Wrappers:** 12px (`rounded-xl`).
- **Timer Blocks, Mini Badges, Status Tags:** 6px to 8px (`rounded-md` / `rounded-lg`) to preserve sharp internal proportions.
- **Floating Action Pills & Category Filter Pills:** Full rounded (`9999px`) for quick tactile scanning.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Flash CTA:** Background `#D70324`, text `#FFFFFF`, font-weight 700. Features 12px border radius, 48px height on mobile. In hover/active states, shifts to `#B8021E` with subtle ambient red glow.
- **Secondary / Claimed:** Background `#F1F3F5`, text `#14213D`, border 1px solid `#E9ECEF`.
- **Outline / Details:** Transparent background, 1.5px border `#14213D`, text `#14213D`.

### Deal & Voucher Cards
- Constructed with surface `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E9ECEF`, and 12px border radius.
- **Top Ribbon / Media Area:** Aspect ratio 1:1 or 4:3 with embedded floating discount tag (e.g., "90% OFF" in `#D70324` fill, white text).
- **Voucher Cutout (Perforation Style):** Symmetrical 12px semi-circle notches masked on horizontal dividers when rendering redeemable digital coupons.
- **Stock Progress Bar:** 6px height track (`#F1F3F5`) with animated fill (`#FF6B35` or `#D70324`) showing remaining quota (e.g., "Tersisa 12%").

### Countdown Timers
- Dark high-contrast configuration: Pill container with `#14213D` background, containing segmented blocks with `#FFFFFF` text in `timer-digit`.
- Segment separators (colon `:`) pulse gently to signal active real-time backend synchronization.

### Form Inputs & Indonesian Localization
- Height 48px, 12px border radius, border 1.5px `#E9ECEF`. Focus ring transitions to `#D70324` with 2px soft outer focus ring (`rgba(215, 3, 36, 0.15)`).
- **Phone Number Field:** Static prefix block containing `+62` with an Indonesian flag icon embedded in a surface-subtle left container (`#F1F3F5`), partitioned by a vertical border from the numeric input area.
- **Price Input / Voucher Amount:** Fixed bold `Rp` prefix aligned to the left of the input canvas.

### Chips & Category Filters
- Pill shape (full circular radius), 36px height.
- Inactive: Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E9ECEF`, text `#14213D`.
- Active: Background `#14213D`, border 1px solid `#14213D`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- Category-specific variant: Overrides active background with respective vertical color tokens (`#FF6B35`, `#E01A67`, `#0066FF`).

### Checkboxes & Selection Controls
- 20px size with 6px corner radius.
- Checked state: Fill `#D70324` with white checkmark glyph.
- Radio buttons: 20px circular with double-ring active stroke in `#D70324`.
